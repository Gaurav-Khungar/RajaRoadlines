Our Story

About Raja Roadlines

Three decades of trust, relationships and results in Maharashtra's freight industry.

Est. Early 1990s

30 years of
delivering trust

Raja Roadlines was founded by Rajeev Khungar in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar (Aurangabad), Maharashtra. What began as a small transport brokerage has grown into one of the most trusted names in the region's freight industry.

Operating from the heart of the Marathwada industrial belt, we have built lasting relationships with manufacturing companies, rubber industries, paper mills, aluminium processors and dozens of fleet owners across India.

Today, Raja Roadlines manages freight for industrial clients across Maharashtra and Gujarat, with a growing presence in Vapi — a major industrial hub on the Mumbai–Surat corridor.

Our Journey

1990s
Founded in Aurangabad

Started as a transport commission agent serving local manufacturing companies in the CSN industrial belt.

2000s
Network Expansion

Built a strong network of fleet owners across Maharashtra. Expanded into ODC, trailer and container freight as core specialisations.

2010s
Industrial Cargo Focus

Became the go-to logistics partner for rubber, paper and aluminium industries. Pan-India routes established.

2020s
Gujarat Expansion

Expanding into Vapi, Gujarat - serving the Mumbai–Vapi–Surat industrial corridor with export, import and ODC freight.
