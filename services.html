Part Load Freight

Why pay for an empty truck? Our part load service consolidates your consignment with other cargo on the same route — so you only pay for the space you use.

Ideal for mid-size consignments that don't fill a full vehicle. Available across all our major routes — CSN, Pune, Mumbai, Nashik, Vapi and beyond.
